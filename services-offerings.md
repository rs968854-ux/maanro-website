# Maanro Service Offerings

Version: November 2026

## Company Core Service
Maanro is an AI Venture Builder for startups & SMEs in India. Our core mission: Help Indian startups and SMEs build, grow, and scale using AI-powered strategies, operational frameworks, and venture builder methodology—locally, without online registration costs or heavy tech infrastructure.

## What We Actually Do
We bridge the gap between AI potential and business reality. Most Indian startups and SMEs know AI exists but dont know how to practically apply it. We provide: 1) Strategic AI implementation, 2) Venture builder methodology, 3) Operational efficiency, 4) Brand and positioning, 5) Growth frameworks.

Includes: AI venture builder assessment, business model canvas, go-to-market strategy framework, brand foundations (logo, tagline, colour palette), 4-week implementation roadmap, email triage setup (anonymous IMAP), Airtable 90-day pipeline base.

## Deliverables:
- Venture builder assessment report (PDF, 3-5 pages)
- Business model canvas (PDF, 2 pages)
- 90-day roadmap (PDF, 1 page)
- Brand basics package (PNG logo files, colour codes, tagline)
- Airtable pipeline base + setup guide
- Email triage configuration guide

## Best For: Pre-revenue startups, concept-stage ventures, first-time founders, SMEs testing AI for the first time

## Deliverables:

## Performance-Based Option (Low-Risk for Founders)
Structure: ₹15,000/month base fee + 5% of monthly revenue growth above baseline.

Baseline: Previous month’s revenue (or ₹0 for pre-revenue startups)

Includes:
- All the strategic guidance, AI advice, and support you’d get in Tier 1
- Monthly check-in call (30 minutes)
- Email support between sessions
- Access to the 7-email template library
- KPI tracking and monthly performance report

How It Works:
1. Month 1: Pay ₹15,000 + 5% of revenue growth above baseline
2. Month 2: Pay ₹15,000 + 5% of revenue growth above baseline
3. Month 3: Pay ₹15,000 + 5% of revenue growth above baseline
4. After 3 months: Renegotiate to Tier 1, 2, or 3 based on results

Example:
- Month 1 revenue: ₹2,00,000 (baseline)
- Month 2 revenue: ₹3,00,000 (growth: ₹1,00,000 above baseline)
- Month 2 fee: ₹15,000 + 5% × ₹1,00,000 = ₹20,000
- Month 3 revenue: ₹4,50,000 (growth: ₹2,50,000 above baseline)
- Month 3 fee: ₹15,000 + 5% × ₹2,50,000 = ₹27,500

Best For: Cash-strapped founders who want results before committing, founders who prefer performance-based pricing, pre-revenue startups

## How Services Flow (Client Journey)

| Stage | What Happens | Typical Tier |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 1. Discovery | Founder contacts Maanro, discusses challenges, goals, budget | Any (usually starts with Brand Foundations or Venture Builder Assessment) |
| 2. Assessment | We conduct the assessment, deliver findings, recommend tier | Usually Tier 1 or Performance-Based Option |
| 3. Onboarding | Engagement signed, setup begins (email, Airtable, branding) | Tier 1, 2, or 3 |
| 4. Implementation | Monthly work: strategy, implementation, optimization, reporting | Tier 1 (monthly), Tier 2 (monthly), Tier 3 (monthly + fractional) |
| 5. Review & Scale (Every 90 Days) | Results analysis, tier adjustment (upgrade/downgrade/stay) | All tiers |
| 6. Ongoing | Continuous improvement, new goals, possibly fractional partner (Tier 3) | Tier 3 primarily |

| Service | What It Is | What It Is Not |

| AI Tool Selection | We recommend specific tools for your business size, budget, and process (not just the trending ones) | Buy all the AI tools |
| Workflow Automation | We design automations using tools like Make, Zapier, n8n, or custom scripts for your specific repeatable tasks | Set up Zapier in 5 minutes |

## Client Onboarding — What Happens First

Step 1: Initial Consultation (15-30 min)
- Free, no-obligation call
- Founder discusses: current challenges, goals, budget, stage
- We assess fit and recommend the right tier/service

Step 2: Service Selection
- Founder chooses: one-time service, Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, or Performance Option
- Engagement letter signed (1-page, 3-month minimum for monthly plans)

Step 3: Setup (Week 1)
- Brand foundations (if not already done)
- Email triage configuration
- Airtable pipeline base
- Initial strategy session (if Tier 2 or 3)

Step 3: First Deliverables (Within 2 Weeks)
- Assessment report
- Roadmap or plan
- Brand assets
- Pipeline base

Step 5: Monthly Work
- Monthly check-in call (30-60 min)
- KPI review and adjustment
- Next month’s focus areas
- Email support between sessions

Step 6: Review & Scale (Every 90 Days)
- Results analysis
- Tier adjustment (upgrade/downgrade/stay)
- Decision: continue, pause, or exit

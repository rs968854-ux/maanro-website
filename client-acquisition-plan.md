# Maanro Client Acquisition Plan

*Last updated: November 2026*

---

## Objective

**Objective:** Find 3 initial clients within 60 days who value AI-powered venture building for Indian startups/SMEs, with budgets matching our Tier 1 or Tier 2 packages.

---
## Target Client Avatars

### Avatar 1: The Pre-Revenue Founder
- **Company Stage:** Idea stage, pre-revenue, first-time founder
- **Budget:** ₹25,000 one-time or ₹8,000/month (Performance Option)
- **Pain Points:** Business idea viability, can't afford expensive consultants, need structured guidance with limited funds
- **Where to Find:** Entrepreneurship cell events, startup incubators, Founder India forums, LinkedIn posts about "just starting up"
- **Messaging Focus:** Low-risk entry — performance option, ₹15,000/month + 5% revenue growth, or ₹25,000 one-time for foundations
- **Primary Service Tier:** Tier 1 or Performance-Based Option

### Avatar 2: The Revenue-Generating Startup
- **Company Stage:** Generating revenue (₹10L-₹50L ARR), growth stage, looking to scale
- **Budget:** ₹50,000 one-time or ₹15,000/month (3-month min)
- **Pain Points:** Growing too fast to keep up, operations chaotic, customer acquisition costs rising, need systematic processes
- **Where to Find:** LinkedIn (growth-stage startup founders, "scaling" posts), SaaS founder groups, industry conferences, trade associations
- **Messaging Focus:** Tier 2 gives operational audit, customer acquisition strategy, KPI dashboard, 7-email template library, advanced Airtable automations
- **Primary Service Tier:** Tier 2

### Avatar 3: The Established SME
- **Company Stage:** ₹50L-₹2Cr+ ARR, looking to scale further or prepare for exit/fundraising
- **Budget:** ₹100,000 one-time or ₹25,000/month + 5% performance fee (minimum 6 months)
- **Pain Points:** Need fractional strategic guidance, preparing for fundraising, looking at new markets, need experienced venture builder as partner, current systems can't scale
- **Where to Find:** LinkedIn (established founder posts, "exit" or "fundraising" topics), industry chambers, private equity networks, business associations
- **Messaging Focus:** Tier 3 gives fractional venture builder (10 hrs/month), board-level guidance, investor readiness, market expansion, custom AI tooling, quarterly strategy reviews
- **Primary Service Tier:** Tier 3

### Performance-Based Option: Risk-Free - ₹15,000/month + 5% growth
**Headline:** Pay for results, not promises

**Key Messages:**
- ₹15,000/month base + 5% of revenue growth above baseline — first 3 months determine continuation
- Pre-revenue startups: baseline is ₹0, so you only pay 5% of first revenue
- Monthly 30-min check-in call + email support between sessions
- KPI tracking and monthly performance report included
- After 3 months: renegotiate to Tier 1, 2, or 3 based on results

**Example:**
- Month 1 revenue: ₹2,00,000 (baseline)
- Month 2 revenue: ₹3,00,000 (growth: ₹1,00,000 above baseline)
- Month 2 fee: ₹15,000 + 5% × ₹1,00,000 = ₹20,000
- Month 3 revenue: ₹4,50,000 (growth: ₹2,50,000 above baseline)
- Month 3 fee: ₹15,000 + 5% × ₹2,50,000 = ₹27,500

**Call-to-Action:**
> Schedule a 15-min discovery call — see if performance-based pricing makes sense for where your company is today.

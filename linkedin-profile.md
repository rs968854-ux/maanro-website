### Personal Introduction

**Maanro** is an **AI Venture Builder for startups & SMEs in India**. We help Indian entrepreneurs and established businesses build, grow, and scale using practical AI, operational frameworks, and venture builder methodology.

**Our Mission:** To bridge the gap between AI potential and business reality for Indian startups and SMEs — providing structured guidance, operational efficiency, and growth frameworks that deliver measurable results.

**Our Approach:**
- **Practical AI:** Not AI hype — targeted AI application for specific business processes (process audit, tool selection, workflow automation, KPI design, strategic implementation)
- **Venture Builder Methodology:** Step-by-step frameworks for building, growing, and scaling ventures
- **Local Implementation:** All services designed for the Indian market, no offshore dependency, works with limited tech infrastructure
- **Measurable Results:** Every engagement includes defined deliverables and KPIs — no ongoing consulting without outcomes
- **Performance-Based Pricing:** First 3 months: ₹15,000/month + 5% of revenue growth above baseline — you only pay for results

**What We Do:**
1. **Build:** Venture builder assessment, business model canvas, go-to-market strategy, branding foundations (logo, tagline, colour palette)
2. **Grow:** Operational efficiency audit, customer acquisition strategy, KPI/dashboard setup, team scalability assessment, email template library
3. **Scale:** Fractional venture builder services (up to 10 hrs/month), board-level strategic guidance, fundraising preparation, market expansion, custom AI tooling, quarterly strategy reviews

**Our Clients:** Pre-revenue startups, revenue-generating startups (₹10L-₹50L ARR), and established SMEs (₹50L-₹2Cr+ ARR) in India.

**Our Differentiator:** Most advisors do one thing — strategy OR branding OR growth analysis. **We do all three**, integrated into a venture building partnership with performance-based pricing that puts founder risk to minimum.

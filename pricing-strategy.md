# Maanro Pricing Strategy

Version: November 2026

## Company Positioning
Maanro is an AI Venture Builder for startups & SMEs in India. Target: startups & SMEs. Market: India-first. Implementation: Local. Brand: Premium, strategic, intelligent growth.

## Pricing Philosophy
Affordable for Indian startups, premium positioning, transparent, flexible, performance-based options.

## Tier 1: ideation - Grow Honour
Price: ₹25,000 one-time or ₹8,000/month (3-month min). Includes: assessment, business model canvas, go-to-market strategy, branding foundations, 4-week roadmap, email triage setup, Airtable pipeline.

## Tier 2: accelerate - Scale with Intelligence
Price: ₹50,000 one-time or ₹15,000/month (3-month min). Includes: everything Tier 1 + AI audit, customer acquisition strategy, monthly KPIs, team scalability, advanced Airtable, 7 email templates.

## Tier 3: transform - Strategic Partner
Price: ₹100,000 one-time or ₹25,000/month (3-month min) + 5% performance fee. Includes: fractional AI venture builder, board-level guidance, fundraising prep, market expansion, custom AI tooling, unlimited email templates, quarterly reviews.

## Performance-Based Option
₹15,000/month base + 5% of monthly revenue growth above baseline. First 3 months commitment.

## One-Time Setup Fees
Brand Foundations: ₹25,000. Venture Builder Assessment: ₹15,000. Email & Pipeline Setup: ₹10,000. Strategy Session (1-day): ₹30,000.

## Payment Terms
Invoice cycle: monthly, due by 5th. Payment: UPI, bank transfer, Paytm, PhonePe. Late payment: 1.5% per month. Cancellation: 30-day notice, prepaid non-refundable.

## Discounts
Pre-revenue startups: 20% off first 3 months. SMEs (₹10Cr+ revenue): 15% on Tier 3. Referral: 10% off first month. Non-profit: 25% off. Annual prepayment: 15% off.

## Pricing Rationale
Indian market alignment, local implementation, tiered approach, performance option, one-time services, discount structure, payment flexibility.

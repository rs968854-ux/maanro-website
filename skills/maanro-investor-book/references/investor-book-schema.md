# Investor Book Schema

## Paragraph Structure

The investor book contains 58 paragraphs organized as follows:

- Paragraphs 1-2: Title and tagline
- Paragraph 3: Details for Investor Review
- Paragraph 4: Executive Summary
- Paragraph 5: Maanro description and operating model
- Paragraph 6: Company Overview (legal name, founder, headquarters, operating model, primary market)
- Paragraph 7: Founder/CEO information
- Paragraph 8: Market definition and 3 avatar segments
- Paragraph 9: Pricing structure (three-tier model)
- Paragraph 10: Brand and Visual Identity
- Paragraph 11: Brand & Visual Identity details
- Paragraph 12: Services & Pricing Strategy (Plan A business, Plan B individual)
- Paragraph 13: Market Opportunity
- Paragraph 14: Team & Organizational Structure
- Paragraph 15: Founder & Personal Brand
- Paragraph 16: Scaling Strategy — Personal Brand Edition
- Paragraph 17: Legal & Compliance Framework
- Paragraph 18: Legal responsibility definitions (Aria as Head of Legal)
- Paragraph 19: Legal authority limitations
- Paragraph 20: External legal support requirements
- Paragraph 21: Privacy & Data Protection (Section 11)
- Paragraph 22: Privacy Acknowledgement (Section 12)
- Paragraph 23: Scaling Strategy — Personal Brand Edition
- Paragraph 24: Dual market serving (Plan A business, Plan B individual)
- Paragraph 24: Revenue projection — combined both plans
- Paragraph 25: Pilot program — both plans
- Paragraph 26: Founder & personal brand
- Paragraph 27: Scaling strategy — personal brand edition

## 11-Column Tracking Sheet Structure

| Column | Header | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Name | Client name |
| 2 | Company | Business name / blank for individual |
| 3 | Stage | Pipeline stage: Prospect → Contacted → Analyzed → Upgraded → Active |
| 4 | Target Tier | Plan A Tier 1/2/3 OR Plan B ₹2,500 |
| 5 | Source | Google / Bhaskar / Directory / Email / Referral / Assn / Portal / Campus / Event |
| 6 | First Contact | Date of first payment (₹1,000 or ₹2,500) |
| 7 | Conversation Status | Analyzed / Report delivered / Upgraded / No upgrade |
| 8 | Next Action (Research) | Specific search query used, Bhaskar filter applied, directory keywords, email subject |
| 9 | Outcome | Analysis delivered / Upgraded to Tier 1 / No upgrade |
| 10 | Notes (Research) | Search parameters, keywords, filters applied, results count, businesses saved |
| 11 | Pilot Tier | Plan A business / Plan B individual / Research phase |

## Plan B — Individual Resume Analysis

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Price | ₹2,500 one-time upfront — single payment, no monthly commitments |
| Target Audience | People looking for jobs, working low-pay jobs, wanting to switch jobs |
| LinkedIn Note | Clients are LinkedIn-active (job-seeking), but Maanro itself does not need LinkedIn registration |
| Deliverable | Full resume/CV analysis — 15-minute analysis, key strengths, 3 improvement areas, industry/role matching, salary benchmark range, skill gap analysis (3 skills), LinkedIn profile optimization (basic: headline, about section, 3 connection templates), 90-day action plan |
| File Format | .docx or .xlsx (not markdown — all 6 files already converted and verified per user directive) |
| Delivery | Within 5 business days of resume receipt + payment |
| No Track Backs | Exactly as agreed: one-and-done engagement. No follow-up emails, no check-ins, no ongoing support. After report delivery + one optional revision (within 7 days, max 30-minute adjustment), engagement is complete. |
| Upsell Opportunity | If client wants more → Offer Plan A Tier 1 (₹28,750) if client is a founder seeking brand strategy. 30-50% of individual clients may upgrade based on need for business brand alignment. |

## Pilot Program Structure

| Program | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 3-5 Client Business Pilot (Months 1-3) | Work free initially until growth demonstrated, then charge standard upfront rates based on milestones (revenue growth ≥20% MoM, client acquisition, operational efficiency, team scalability, value perception ≥7/10) |
| First 10 Individual Clients (Plan B) at ₹2,500 | Introductory pricing to validate service demand and gather feedback. First 10 clients at ₹2,500 introductory rate. After 10 clients, rate review: ₹3,000 or ₹4,000 based on demand and deliverable refinement. |
| Milestone Criteria for Both Plans | Revenue growth ≥20% MoM (if applicable), Client acquisition (number of new clients acquired through Maanro efforts), Operational efficiency improvements, Team scalability assessment (if hiring), Client value perception ≥7/10 |

## Research Channel Mix

| Channel | % Allocation | Description |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Google Search | 30% | 10 keywords executed, 30+ businesses identified in incognito window; expansion: +5 keywords to reach 15 total |
| Startup India Bhaskar | 25% | 600K+ community, 89,562 DPIIT recognised startups; BHASKAR platform: one-stop digital platform for ecosystem stakeholders |
| Direct Network & Referrals | 20% | Existing contacts, word-of-mouth, personal network in India business community; tracking: referral_source, referral_name, warmth_level, intro_date |
| B2B Directories | 10% | Justdial, IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Sulekha; free search; paid listing optional |
| Government Portals | 10% | MSME Udyam, state startup policies, Make in India; all free; no online registration costs for Maanro |
| Industry Associations | 5% | FICCI, CII sector directories; member search filters by company size, sector, location |
| Cold Email Outreach | 5% | Using existing Gmail REST API infrastructure (maanroadvertising@gmail.com, 7-template library) |
| Campus/University Connect | 3% | IITs, IIMs, state universities; search: site:*.ac.in "entrepreneurship cell" |
| Trade Shows & Events | 2% | Manufacturing expos, startup galas, SME summits; physical/local attendance acceptable |

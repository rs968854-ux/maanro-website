## Google Search Operators (Always-On)

- `site:*.com "India"` — Indian companies with .com domains
- `-linkedin` — Exclude LinkedIn noise (per user constraint: 'we can not use linkdin as we are not registerd')
- `intitle:"advisor" OR "consultant" OR "strategist"` — Title focus
- `"India" "advisor" "pricing" OR "fees"` — Budget-aware filtering
- `"India" "startup" "advisor"` — Startup-specific filtering

## Incognito Window Methodology

- Always search in incognito/private window to avoid result personalization
- Record findings in 11-column tracking sheet (Column 10: Notes - Research)
- Note the date of each search session
- Do not rely on search history; each session starts fresh

## Business Data Recording Format

For each business found, record in tracking sheet:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Business Name | Official company name |
| Website URL | URL of official website |
| Location | City/State/Region |
| Brief Description | 1-2 sentence description of what the company does |
| Google Reviews Count | Number of reviews (if available) |
| Stage Match | Avatar alignment: Curious Founder (<10L ARR), Growth-Seeking SME (10L-50L ARR), Established Player (50L-2Cr+ ARR) |
| Contact Info | Visible email/phone on website (if any) |
| Active Website | Yes/No — does the site appear active (fresh content, working links) |

## Multi-Channel Research Integration

### Google Search (30% effort)
- 10 keywords executed, 30+ businesses identified
- Record in tracking sheet with full data fields
- Filter for: active websites, visible contact info, stage match
- Do not rely on search history; each session starts fresh in incognito

### Startup India Bhaskar (25% effort)
- Browse 600K+ community, 89,562 DPIIT recognised startups
- Use DPIIT filters: stage (early/growth), sector (services/manufacturing), location (Delhi/NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad)
- BHASKAR platform: one-stop digital platform for ecosystem stakeholders
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Bhaskar"

### Direct Network & Referrals (20% effort)
- Existing contacts, word-of-mouth, personal network in India business community
- Track in tracking sheet: referral_source, referral_name, warmth_level, intro_date
- No online registration required; leverage existing relationships

### B2B Directories (10% effort)
- Justdial: Search with city filter and rating (4+ stars)
- IndiaMART: Category: Services, Company size: 1-50 employees
- TradeIndia: Industry: All; Location: India
- Sulekha: Location: Add pincode; Verify: Mobile No.
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Directory"

### Government Portals (10% effort)
- MSME Udyam: State-wise search (Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Delhi); Enterprise type: Micro/Small/Medium
- State startup policies: "Know Your State/UT" from Startup India portal
- Make in India: Sector: Manufacturing; Stage: Growth
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Portal"

### Industry Associations (5% effort)
- FICCI: Member directory filter by industry, company size
- CII: Member search by company size, sector
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Association"

### Cold Email Outreach (5% effort)
- Using existing Gmail REST API infrastructure (maanroadvertising@gmail.com, 7-template library)
- Gmail search operators: `subject:"brand growth" from:maanroadvertising@gmail.com`
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Email"

### Campus/University Connect (3% effort)
- search: site:*.ac.in "entrepreneurship cell"
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Campus"

### Trade Shows & Events (2% effort)
- Manufacturing expos, startup galas, SME summits
- Physical/local attendance acceptable
- Record in tracking sheet with source: "Event"

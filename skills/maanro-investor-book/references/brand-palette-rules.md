# Brand Palette Rules

## Core Colours (Permanent)

| Colour | HEX | Purpose | Restrictions |
|--------|-----|---------|-------------|
| Obsidian Black | #080808 | Authority, confidence, intelligence, sophistication | Permanent core colour; do not introduce burgundy, bronze, or gold into primary identity |
| Growth Green | #32CD32 | Innovation, growth, technology, progress | Permanent core colour; do not use multiple competing neon colours |

## Supporting Colour Palette (6 Colours)

| Colour | HEX | Purpose | Usage |
|--------|-----|---------|-------|
| Pure Black | #000000 | Logo backgrounds and maximum contrast | Use for logo backgrounds only |
| Graphite | #171A17 | Cards, panels and interface backgrounds | Use for secondary sections and subtle surfaces |
| Deep Forest | #263326 | Secondary sections and subtle green surfaces | Use for secondary green surfaces |
| Soft Lime | #A7ECA7 | Gentle highlights and secondary graphics | Use for gentle highlights only |
| Cloud White | #F4F7F4 | Primary text and light backgrounds | Use for primary text and lightest backgrounds |
| Slate Grey | #A1AAA1 | Secondary text captions and borders | Use for secondary text captions and borders only |

## Colour Allocation — Visual Balance (Recommended, Not Rigid)

- 60% Obsidian Black — Primary dominance, authority, sophistication
- 30% Growth Green — Secondary accent, innovation, growth, technology, progress
- 10% Cloud White — Light backgrounds, primary text, contrast, readability

## Official Logo Colour Rules

| Logo Element | HEX Colour | Meaning |
|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Background | Obsidian Black #080808 | Provides contrast and premium appearance |
| MAAN element | Cloud White #F4F7F4 | Main wordmark colour |
| RO element | Growth Green #32CD32 | Secondary wordmark colour |
| Growth arrow | Growth Green #32CD32 | Represents growth direction |
| Growth bars | Growth Green #32CD32 | Represents ROI and growth direction |
| Tagline | Cloud White #F4F7F4 | Contrast against darker wordmark |
| Supporting graphics | Growth Green or Graphite | Must not compete with wordmark readability |

## Colour Usage Restrictions (Always-On)

1. Obsidian Black #080808 and Growth Green #32CD32 are the permanent core colours
2. Do not introduce burgundy, bronze, or gold into the primary identity
3. Do not use multiple competing neon colours
4. Keep gradients and glow effects out of the official flat logo
5. Maintain strong text contrast and readability at all sizes
6. Use supporting colours consistently, not randomly
7. Do not alter the logo's colour assignments without formal approval
8. Minimum text size: ensure 12pt minimum for body text with 4.5:1 contrast ratio

## Triggers (When to Use These Rules)

- Creating or updating brand-guidelines.docx
- Designing new marketing materials or website
- Reviewing logo usage across platforms
- Ensuring accessibility compliance (contrast ratio)
- Training new team members on brand standards

## Notes

- These rules are always-on; they apply to every instance of brand material creation
- 60/30/10 allocation is recommended but not rigid; the priority is maintaining Obsidian Black as dominant, Growth Green as secondary, and Cloud White for contrast
- Logo colour assignments must not be altered without formal approval
- Accessibility: maintain 4.5:1 contrast ratio for normal text, 3:1 for large text per WCAG guidelines

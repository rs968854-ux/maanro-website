# Maanro Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0

## Company Identity
- Name: Maanro
- Tagline: Grow with Honour. Scale with Intelligence.
- Focus: AI Venture Builder for startups & SMEs in India
- Market: English, India-first
- Implementation: Local

## Brand Personality
Maanro should communicate:
- Premium business positioning
- Strategic thinking and intelligent growth
- Trust, discipline and integrity
- Creative capability backed by commercial thinking

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo - Horizontal
- File: maanro-Luxury-Advertising-Logo.png (1672 x 941, 16:9)
- Composition: MAAN in Oxblood Burgundy #240710 (left), RO in Champagne Bronze #B78A55 (right)
- Background: Obsidian Black #100D0F
- Minimum size: 400px width
- Clear space: 1x text height around logo

### Vertical Logo - 9:16
- File: Maanro Advertising_Grow with Honour.png (941 x 1672, 9:16)
- Composition: MAAN in #240710 (top, ~60%), RO in #B78A55 (bottom, ~40%)
- Background: Obsidian Black #100D0F
- Minimum display: 300px height
- Use case: Reels, phone screens, vertical videos

## Logo Colours

| Colour | HEX | Purpose | Rules |
|--------|-------|---------|-------|
| Oxblood Burgundy | #240710 | MAAN | Fixed — do not change |
| Champagne Bronze | #B78A55 | RO | Fixed — do not change |
| Obsidian Black | #100D0F | Background | Fixed — do not substitute |
| Warm Ivory | #E8D6B8 | Tagline text | Fixed — tagline only |
| Royal Wine | #681D32 | Secondary accent | Optional, supporting elements only |

## Logo Donts
- Do not recolour MAAN or RO
- Do not add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
- Do not rearrange MAAN and RO order
- Do not thin or thicken the bronze stripe proportion
- Do not place logo on busy backgrounds without clear space
- Do not use on white/light backgrounds without Obsidian Black background

## Tagline

**Tagline:** "Grow with Honour. Scale with Intelligence."
- Font colour: Warm Ivory #E8D6B8
- Placement: Below the logo wordmark, centred
- Spacing: At least 10px below the logo, at least 10px above other text
- Do not modify: wording, punctuation, or word order
- Do not use without the logo present (minimum integration)
- Use case: Website footers, email signatures, social media bios, marketing materials

## Typography
- Tagline: "Grow with Honour. Scale with Intelligence."
- Colour: Warm Ivory #E8D6B8
- Recommended fonts: DejaVu Sans Bold, or any clean sans-serif with good readability at small sizes
- Do not use decorative, script, or display fonts for body copy
- Supporting text: maintain professional, readable fonts reflecting premium, strategic positioning

## Social Media Usage

### Profile Pictures
- Recommended: Vertical 9:16 logo (941 x 1672)
- Safe zone: Keep all critical elements (MAAN, RO) within the central 600px x 600px area
- Do not crop MAAN or RO out of the composition

### Cover/Header Images
- Recommended: Use Obsidian Black #100D0F as background colour
- Accent: May include thin Champagne Bronze #B78A55 stripe
- Tagline: May appear in Warm Ivory #E8D6B8
- Do not use competing colours (neon, bright gradients, etc.)

### Post Content
- Main visual: Maanro logo or product-related imagery in brand palette
- Text colour: Prefer Warm Ivory #E8D6B8 or Oxblood Burgundy #240710
- Background: Obsidian Black #100D0F for premium feel, or white with brand accent stripes
- Do not mix multiple brand colours haphazardly — follow the palette table

## Marketing Materials

### Business Cards
- Front: Logo (horizontal 16:9) top-left, contact info centre, tagline bottom-right
- Back: Obsidian Black #100D0F background, Champagne Bronze #B78A55 horizontal accent stripe across bottom
- Text: Warm Ivory #E8D6B8 for body text, Oxblood Burgundy #240710 for headings

### Pitch Decks
- Title slide: Maanro logo (vertical 9:16 or horizontal 16:9, full-colour)
- Colour scheme: Use the palette table — 60% Obsidian Black, 30% Oxblood Burgundy, 10% Champagne Bronze
- Do not use more than 3 brand colours in any single slide

### Website
- Primary background: Obsidian Black #100D0F
- Accent colours: Oxblood Burgundy #240710 (headings, buttons), Champagne Bronze #B78A55 (borders, highlights), Warm Ivory #E8D6B8 (body text, dividers)
- Do not use full-colour photographs without overlaying with the brand palette
- Do maintain consistent colour usage across all pages
